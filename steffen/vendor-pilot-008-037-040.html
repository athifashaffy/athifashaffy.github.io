037 · Jumping leg lunge

Relatio reference

The movement is a repeated single-leg knee-drive hop: the free heel folds behind, the knee drives forward/up, and the supporting foot hops and lands. It is not an alternating deep lunge jump.

Relatio’s on-video label is 32. This source demonstrates one working side.

Promising movement match

Gym Visual · Jumping Single Leg Lunge

The preview shows the corresponding knee-drive hop, free heel tucked behind and coordinated arm swing. It is a much closer candidate than a standard jumping lunge. The other working side is not established by this preview.

Exercise Animatic · Jumping Lunge

040 · Push-up side plank

Relatio reference

Floor push-ups interspersed with alternating side-plank rotations and an upward arm reach. Compare the hand transfer, shoulder rotation, foot roll and push-ups between sides.

Relatio’s on-video label is 29. The clip begins during a side-plank phase.

Decision for this pilot

Prioritise Gym Visual for these three movements. First confirm whether the anatomical visual style is acceptable. It differs from our clothed cartoon Coach and does not preserve the Coach’s smile, blinking or clothing.

  • These clips have not been bought, integrated or approved.
  • Before adoption: inspect complete cycles and contact details, match cadence, prepare the agreed repetition counts and numeric circular clock, and review all final frames and joins.
  • 008: ten repetitions. 037: five on each working side, with the other-side asset still to establish. 040: five individual alternating side-plank repetitions, not five left-right pairs.
