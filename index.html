01 · About

About me

I am a software developer and founder originally from Sri Lanka, now based in Canada. I build software end to end: on-device AI, autonomous agents, clinical-grade mobile apps, industrial telemetry systems, and the web tooling around them.

Through The Alpha Nova Inc., I work on applied AI, IoT, automation, health-tech, and industrial software. A large part of my work is the COGITO family of mental health apps built with the clinical neuropsychology team at UKE Hamburg, alongside autonomy and perception work for drones and mining-grade industrial systems.

I also teach part-time at Cambrian College and mentor students and first-time builders through hackathons and developer clubs.

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, applied AI, and practical innovation.

Autonomous Multimodal Drone Agent

Autonomy

A fully autonomous agent for communication-denied UAV operations, built for the CAF Red Team Hackathon. It ingests live video and telemetry, interprets embedded visual puzzles with a dual-speed perception pipeline (fast CV loop plus slower reasoning loop), and issues joystick control commands with zero human intervention and no GPS. Ships with its own mock simulator, a black-box recorder, and a Next.js mission-control dashboard.

Cryptographic Supply Chain Provenance

Security

A provenance system for verifying “Made in Canada” and “Product of Canada” claims in the drone supply chain. Every supplier contribution is an Ed25519-signed attestation referencing its upstream attestations, forming a tamper-evident DAG from raw materials to finished product. The system walks the chain, verifies every signature, computes Canadian content percentages per Competition Bureau rules, and flags anomalies. Third place, Red Team DefTech Verified Canadian Supply Chains Hackathon, Ottawa.

  • Ed25519
  • Cryptographic attestation
  • DAG
  • Compliance
