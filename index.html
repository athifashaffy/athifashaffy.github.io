01 · About

About me

I am a software developer and founder originally from Sri Lanka, now based in Sudbury, Ontario. I moved to Canada as a first-generation immigrant and built my local network by creating opportunities instead of waiting for them.

Through The Alpha Nova Inc., I work on applied AI, IoT, automation, health-tech, and industrial software projects. Alongside my technical work, I organize developer events, teach part-time at Cambrian College, and help students, researchers, professors, and companies collaborate on real-world technology.

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, community building, and practical innovation.
